# Evaluation Rubric: 1940s Comic Book

## STYLE FIDELITY (Weight: 35%)

### 1. Flat Color (Pass/Fail — gate check)
- [ ] Are colors flat and solid with NO Ben-Day dots or halftone patterns?
- [ ] Are shadows rendered as single flat black shapes, not hatched or cross-hatched?
- **FAIL if:** Ben-Day dots are visible anywhere → this is pop art, not comic style

### 2. Linework Quality
- [ ] Bold black ink outlines with varied line weight
- [ ] Clean cel art look — shapes are drawn and filled, not painted
- [ ] Lines feel hand-inked, not digitally perfect

### 3. Color Palette
- [ ] Restricted to 5-7 ink colors: red, blue, yellow, flesh tone, black
- [ ] Includes flesh tones and warm browns (hair, skin) — warmer than pop art
- [ ] Background shows warm aged-newsprint/cream tone
- [ ] No gradients, no blended colors

### 4. Era Authenticity
- [ ] Resembles 1940s Golden-Age comics (Superman, Captain America)
- [ ] No modern digital coloring or glossy rendering
- [ ] Flesh-and-ink feel, not gallery art

## COMPOSITION (Weight: 30%)

### 5. Cover Furniture (cover mode)
- [ ] Masthead present with three stacked lines + lightning bolts
- [ ] Subhead strip, price box, issue/date corner
- [ ] Approval badge with robot logo
- [ ] Starburst title banner in lower third
- [ ] All text matches specified wording — no invented text

### 6. Panel Structure (story mode)
- [ ] Single saturated background field per panel
- [ ] Narration caption boxes present and correctly styled
- [ ] Speed/action lines used effectively
- [ ] Panel borders are clean black lines

### 7. Focus & Simplification
- [ ] Detail only on kid(s) and robot — everything else simplified to flat color
- [ ] No cluttered rooms, extra props, detailed backgrounds
- [ ] Faces read as illustrated comic characters, not identifiable people

## TECHNICAL QUALITY (Weight: 20%)

### 8. AI Artifacts
- [ ] No mangled hands, merged faces, impossible anatomy
- [ ] No distorted text or broken lettering
- [ ] Clean, readable cover elements

### 9. Robot Fidelity
- [ ] Robots rendered at low detail matching character level
- [ ] Physically plausible grippers, pushers, hooks, or arms
- [ ] Not over-detailed or photorealistic

### 10. Text Rules
- [ ] Only specified text rendered — no invented posters, signs, slogans
- [ ] Background surfaces are blank
- [ ] No floating text or labels

## CONTENT ACCURACY (Weight: 15%)

### 11. Subject Correctness
- [ ] Kids and robots visible and correctly depicted
- [ ] Action matches described scene
- [ ] Proportions are comic-realistic, not chibi or oversized

### 12. Mood
- [ ] Heroic, optimistic, aspirational
- [ ] Kids proud of what they built
- [ ] Exciting, competitive energy

## SCORING

- **PASS:** ≥ 9/12 AND flat color gate check passed
- **REVISE:** 6-8/12 or style mostly correct but some issues
- **FAIL:** < 6/12 or halftone dots visible
