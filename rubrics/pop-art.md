# Evaluation Rubric: Pop Art

## STYLE FIDELITY (Weight: 35%)

### 1. Ben-Day Dots (Pass/Fail — gate check)
- [ ] Are Ben-Day dot halftone patterns visible throughout the image?
- [ ] Do dots appear in skin tones, backgrounds, skies, AND shading areas?
- [ ] Are dots precise and regular (mechanical halftone), not messy or random?
- **FAIL if:** Image uses flat solid color shading without dots → this is comic style, not pop art

### 2. Linework Quality
- [ ] Bold black contour lines are present and crisp
- [ ] Lines are graphic and hard-edged, not sketchy or painterly
- [ ] Line weight is consistent and confident

### 3. Color Palette
- [ ] Limited to primary colors: red, yellow, blue, black, white
- [ ] No brown/tan flesh tones — skin is rendered in dot-shaded primaries
- [ ] No pastels, muted colors, or extended palette

### 4. Overall Era Authenticity
- [ ] Image resembles a 1960s Lichtenstein museum painting, not a modern illustration
- [ ] No digital-art "sheen" or contemporary rendering techniques
- [ ] Feels like a gallery piece, not a comic book

## COMPOSITION (Weight: 30%)

### 5. Dramatic Impact
- [ ] Strong, high-contrast graphic composition
- [ ] Sense of drama, action, or intense emotion
- [ ] Eye is drawn to faces first, then action

### 6. Background Treatment
- [ ] Background is minimal: flat color or dotted field
- [ ] No cluttered rooms, detailed environments, or extraneous props
- [ ] Negative space is used purposefully

### 7. Figure Placement
- [ ] Characters are appropriately sized and positioned for the composition type
- [ ] All children are outside the playfield perimeter (arena scenes)
- [ ] Robots are visible and identifiable

## TECHNICAL QUALITY (Weight: 20%)

### 8. AI Artifacts
- [ ] No obvious AI glitches: mangled hands, extra fingers, merged faces
- [ ] No distorted robot parts or impossible mechanical configurations
- [ ] Faces are readable and consistent

### 9. Text & Lettering
- [ ] Speech balloons, if present, are burst/starburst style
- [ ] Sound effects, if present, are limited to at most one (WHAM!, CLASH!, etc.)
- [ ] No floating text, slogans, or wall lettering
- [ ] Any code visible ONLY on a laptop screen facing the viewer

### 10. Robot Fidelity
- [ ] Robots resemble real student-built robots, not battlebots or mechs
- [ ] Visible: Micro:bit boards, two-wheel chassis, batteries, wiring, servos
- [ ] Proportions match real educational robots

## CONTENT ACCURACY (Weight: 15%)

### 11. Subject Correctness
- [ ] Correct number and type of subjects (kids, robots, game pieces)
- [ ] Game pieces match real competition elements (foam balls, colored cubes)
- [ ] Controllers (if shown) match real Micro:bit joysticks from the rear

### 12. Mood & Tone
- [ ] Celebratory, electric, exciting — not violent or militaristic
- [ ] Celebrates ingenuity, engineering, teamwork
- [ ] No weapons, lasers, explosions, or military imagery

## SCORING

Each item scored 0-1 (0=missing/wrong, 0.5=partially, 1=present/correct).
- **PASS:** ≥ 9/12 AND Ben-Day dots gate check passed
- **REVISE:** 6-8/12 or dots present but weak
- **FAIL:** < 6/12 or dots absent
