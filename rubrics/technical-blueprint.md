# Evaluation Rubric: Technical Blueprint

## STYLE FIDELITY (Weight: 35%)

### 1. Blueprint Color Scheme (Pass/Fail — gate check)
- [ ] Deep blue background with white/light-cyan linework
- [ ] True blueprint aesthetic: white lines on blue paper
- **FAIL if:** Full color, wrong background color, or inverted (blue lines on white)

### 2. Linework Quality
- [ ] Clean, precise drafting-quality lines
- [ ] Consistent thin line weight with slightly thicker outlines for major forms
- [ ] Straight edges, perfect circles, measured proportions
- [ ] No sketchy, hand-drawn, or artistic line quality

### 3. Rendering Style
- [ ] Wireframe only — no filled surfaces, no shading, no gradients
- [ ] Objects shown as line drawings (orthographic, isometric, or wireframe)
- [ ] No photorealistic rendering, no 3D surfaces, no textures

### 4. Drafting Conventions
- [ ] Technical callouts with leader lines present
- [ ] Dimension marks, part numbers, or notes in technical font
- [ ] Title block present (lower right corner)
- [ ] Optional: faint background grid

## COMPOSITION (Weight: 30%)

### 5. View Selection
- [ ] Appropriate engineering views (orthographic multi-view, isometric, exploded)
- [ ] Views are properly aligned and scaled
- [ ] Consistent projection throughout

### 6. Annotation Quality
- [ ] Callouts point clearly to specific features
- [ ] No floating, purposeless annotations
- [ ] Text is readable technical font (all-caps, monospace feel)

### 7. Layout Balance
- [ ] Subject centered with appropriate margins
- [ ] Annotations arranged around the perimeter without crowding
- [ ] Title block properly positioned and scaled

## TECHNICAL QUALITY (Weight: 20%)

### 8. AI Artifacts
- [ ] No broken lines, warped geometry, or impossible projections
- [ ] Consistent line quality throughout
- [ ] Grid lines (if present) are straight and evenly spaced

### 9. Information Design
- [ ] The drawing communicates useful information about the subject
- [ ] Not just decorative — actually reads as a technical document
- [ ] Views and annotations work together to explain the subject

### 10. Cleanliness
- [ ] No stray marks, smudges, or noise
- [ ] Crisp, clean blueprint reproduction quality
- [ ] No mixed styles (part blueprint, part illustration)

## CONTENT ACCURACY (Weight: 15%)

### 11. Subject Correctness
- [ ] Correct subjects rendered as technical drawings
- [ ] Robots show engineering detail appropriate to real student robots
- [ ] No fantasy or sci-fi elements presented as real engineering

### 12. Technical Plausibility
- [ ] The depicted engineering is physically plausible
- [ ] Dimensions and proportions are believable
- [ ] Parts and assemblies make mechanical sense

## SCORING

- **PASS:** ≥ 9/12 AND blueprint color scheme gate check passed
- **REVISE:** 6-8/12 or correct scheme but drafting quality issues
- **FAIL:** < 6/12 or wrong color scheme
