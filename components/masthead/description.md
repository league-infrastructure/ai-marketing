# Masthead (burst title treatment)

The big headline-word treatment used at the top of a card — a single dramatic word (e.g.
"ENGINEERS", "AT HIS COMMAND") in bold comic display lettering on a jagged burst.

## Layout
- A **jagged starburst / explosion banner** (blue with a red or cream inner shape) behind the
  headline word.
- The word set in **huge, bold, 3-D block comic capitals** with a **heavy black outline** and
  an extruded/drop-shadow depth.

## Lettering & color
- Fill is usually **signal red** (`~#CC1616`) or cream, with a thick black keyline and a solid
  extrude for the 3-D block feel.
- Burst is **steel-blue / navy** with cream and red accents; energetic and high-contrast.
- Matches the "At His Command" masthead and the "ENGINEERS" title on the postcard.
