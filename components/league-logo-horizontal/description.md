# League Logo — Horizontal (structure)

The horizontal lock-up of the League of Amazing Programmers wordmark: three centered, stacked
lines of bold condensed caps with flanking lightning bolts. This describes the STRUCTURE
(element placement and proportion) only — color and finish come from whichever style the
component is rendered in.

## Elements & placement (top to bottom, centered)
1. **Line 1 — "THE LEAGUE OF":** top line. "THE LEAGUE" in condensed caps, with a small "OF"
   centered on its own short line just below it (between "THE LEAGUE" and "AMAZING"). A small
   lightning bolt sits just outside each end of this line (one left, one right).
2. **Line 2 — "AMAZING":** the dominant line — the largest lettering, condensed caps, centered,
   with a slight energetic upward tilt. A small lightning bolt flanks each side of it.
3. **Line 3 — "PROGRAMMERS":** bottom line, condensed caps, centered, narrower than "AMAZING".

## Structural notes
- The lines are stacked with CLEAR vertical separation — no line's lettering touches or
  overlaps the line above or below. In particular, leave a distinct gap between the small "OF"
  and "AMAZING" so "OF" never cuts into or touches the tops of the "AMAZING" letters
  (especially the "Z"). Nudge "AMAZING" down slightly if needed to keep that gap open.
- "AMAZING" is roughly 1.6–2× the cap height of the two outer lines and is the visual anchor of
  the mark.
- There are four small lightning bolts total — two flanking Line 1 and two flanking Line 2 —
  drawn as short angled sparks kept clear of the type.
- The overall footprint is wider than tall (roughly 2.2 : 1), reading as a compact corner mark.
- All lettering is bold and condensed with heavy keylines; exact fills, colors, and finish are
  defined by the render style, not here.
