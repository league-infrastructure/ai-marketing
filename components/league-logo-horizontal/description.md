# League Logo — Horizontal (structure)

The horizontal lock-up of the League of Amazing Programmers wordmark: three centered, stacked
lines of bold condensed caps with flanking lightning bolts. This describes the STRUCTURE
(element placement and proportion) only — color and finish come from whichever style the
component is rendered in.

## Elements & placement (top to bottom, centered)
1. **Line 1 — "THE LEAGUE OF" (all on ONE line):** "LEAGUE" is the main word of this line,
   centered and the largest word on the line. The small word "THE" sits immediately to its left
   and the small word "OF" immediately to its right, all on the same line. Each small word
   ("THE" and "OF") is bracketed by a SHORT horizontal bar directly above it and directly below
   it. A small lightning bolt sits just outside each end of the whole line (one left, one right).
2. **Line 2 — "AMAZING":** the dominant line — the largest lettering, condensed caps, centered,
   with a slight energetic upward tilt. A small lightning bolt flanks each side of it.
3. **Line 3 — "PROGRAMMERS":** bottom line, condensed caps, centered, narrower than "AMAZING".

## Structural notes
- The wordmark has exactly THREE text lines — "THE LEAGUE OF", "AMAZING", "PROGRAMMERS". The
  word "OF" appears ONLY ONCE, on the top line; there is NO separate standalone "OF" line.
- The whole mark is enclosed in a curved banner/shield frame: a rounded rectangle whose top and
  bottom edges arch gently upward, drawn as a double outline (a thin inner rule just inside a
  heavier outer rule). All elements sit inside this frame.
- The ONLY horizontal bars in the mark are the short ones bracketing "THE" and "OF" (one above
  and one below each small word). There is NO horizontal rule between "AMAZING" and
  "PROGRAMMERS", and NO rules running out to the left/right sides of "OF".
- The lines are stacked with clear vertical separation — no line's lettering touches or overlaps
  the line above or below.
- "AMAZING" is roughly 1.6–2× the cap height of the two outer lines and is the visual anchor of
  the mark.
- Small lightning bolts flank the mark (one at each end of the top line and of the "PROGRAMMERS"
  line), drawn as short angled sparks kept clear of the type.
- The overall footprint is wider than tall (roughly 2.2 : 1), reading as a compact corner mark.
- All lettering is bold and condensed with heavy keylines; exact fills, colors, and finish are
  defined by the render style, not here.
