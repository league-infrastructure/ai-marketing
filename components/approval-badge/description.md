# Approval Badge (robot seal)

A circular "seal of approval" stamp — styled after a vintage comics-code / authority seal —
featuring the League robot.

## Layout
- A heavy **black ring** forming a circular stamp.
- **Center:** a bust/front view of the League's student-built robot (the yellow Micro:bit-style
  robot with the pixel-face screen), kept flat and iconic.
- **Ring text:** curved caps following the ring — e.g. **"APPROVED BY"** along the inside top
  and **"THE CREATORS CODE AUTHORITY"** along the inside bottom.

## Color
- House orange-blue palette: cream/white ring fill, black keyline and ring text, orange and
  steel-blue accents on the robot, a yellow spark or chest bolt.
