Robot Riot Email Banner

Robot Riot email-header banner — same event/cast as the Robot Riot postcard, recomposed as a wide letterboxed 3:1 banner with the League corner logo

A Robot Riot banner scene composed so ALL content sits within the TOP THIRD of the 1536x1024 canvas (a band roughly 1536x340px along the top edge), as if built for a wide letterboxed crop; the bottom two-thirds of the canvas is a plain, unimportant continuation of the golden-amber arena floor, since it will be cropped away and discarded. Within that top band: the headline ROBOT RIOT runs across the very top in a clean, bold, chunky demolition-derby logo style, signal-red fill with heavy black outline, sized with a clear margin so no letter touches the top edge. Below the headline, ONLY two kid drivers total (not four, not a crowd) — one near the left edge, one near the right edge — lean in gripping joystick controllers, excited and focused, heads and shoulders fully inside the band. At the center of the band, two Cutebot robots (recognizable educational-robotics builds, wheels and attachments like a blade/ramp/gripper/scoop) collide head-on in a flat impact starburst with jagged crack lines, positioned low enough in the band that no part of either robot is cropped by the band's lower edge. In the top-left corner of the full canvas, tucked tight into the corner: the League diagonal corner-banner logo, small, clear of the headline and the left driver.
