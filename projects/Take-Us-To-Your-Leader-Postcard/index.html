Take Us To Your Leader Postcard

Advocate campaign postcard, retro flying-saucer gag. A little spaceship has landed and opened in a warm office; the 'aliens' who step out are recognizably a LEAGUE KID and a friendly HUMANOID ROBOT. They greet a bemused adult and ask to be taken to the leader. Second read: the 'aliens' are your kid and the robot they built — and they want to meet your boss. The ask is one warm introduction.

On the landscaped central lawn of a sleek modern corporate research campus (glass-and-concrete engineering building simplified in the background, grassy plaza, sidewalks, a few trees and flowers, with the word \"TechnoCorp\" mounted on the building in the same clean bold sans-serif signage lettering style previously used for QUALCOMM), a classic 1950s flying saucer has just landed with its ramp lowered. A confident young inventor (about 13) has stepped out beside a friendly humanoid robot companion; the child stands with one hand extended toward a passing engineer, the robot politely attentive just behind. The engineer, carrying a briefcase and notebook on his way into work, has stopped on the sidewalk — surprised and amused, not frightened.
