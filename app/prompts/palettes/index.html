Orange & Blue

orange-blue.md

A warm-dominant, high-energy orange-and-blue complementary palette extracted from the "At His Command" comic cover: a glowing golden-amber field carries the image, burnt-orange shadows model the warm forms, and cool steel-blue and deep navy provide the complementary accent.

Balance: ~70% warm / 30% cool — orange and gold dominate large areas; blue is the accent that frames, cools shadows, and makes warms pop.

Circuit Teal

circuit-teal.md

An invented cool-dominant palette — the inverse balance of orange-blue. Teal, steel blue, and deep navy own the image, fresh green threads through as a supporting cool, and League orange is the single warm spark that makes the coolness pop.

Balance: ~70% cool / 30% warm-neutral, with orange as a deliberate <10% spark.

Midnight Arcade

midnight-arcade.md

An invented dark-mode neon palette built from the League's own accent hues: deep indigo-navy and near-black backgrounds lit by electric orange, cyan, violet, and lime. High energy, high contrast — a nighttime arcade / game-screen / hackathon glow.

Balance: dark navy/black fields dominate (60–70%); neons are concentrated light sources against that darkness. Orange remains the lead neon.

Brand Guide

brand-guide.md

The core palette from the League's 2025 Website Style Guide: bold League orange over cream backgrounds, anchored by a deep navy for headings and a soft charcoal for text. Extended with a related blue, green, and red so the accents harmonize.

Balance: orange + cream own the large areas; navy anchors headings; blue/green/red accents used sparingly and one at a time.

Website

website.md

The official League of Amazing Programmers website palette, taken directly from the design tokens on jointheleague.org. Clean, bright, and modern: League orange leads, warm peach and cream carry large surfaces, near-black text anchors, and saturated subject-accent colors provide vivid highlights.

Balance: orange + warm neutrals dominate surfaces; saturated accents used sparingly for highlights, tags, and focal pops.
