# Evaluation Rubric: Manga

## STYLE FIDELITY (Weight: 35%)

### 1. Black & White (Pass/Fail — gate check)
- [ ] Is the image truly black and white with NO color?
- [ ] No sepia, no tinting, no spot color
- **FAIL if:** Any color is present

### 2. Linework Quality
- [ ] Clean varied-weight ink linework
- [ ] Heavy blacks for silhouette, thin lines for detail
- [ ] Broken contour lines where light/motion dissolves form
- [ ] Not perfectly enclosed, evenly rendered — forms break into negative space

### 3. Screentone Usage
- [ ] Screentone present but SPARING: 10-20% of image max
- [ ] Types: flat grey fields, dot tone, diagonal tone, small hatching areas
- [ ] No airbrushed gradients or painterly shadows
- [ ] Screentone is deliberate, not sloppy

### 4. Manga Visual Grammar
- [ ] Sharp, angular composition with strong diagonals
- [ ] Cropped forms, aggressive silhouettes
- [ ] Space suggested through black shapes + white gaps + speed-line geometry
- [ ] Not rounded, enclosed, Western-comic feel

## COMPOSITION (Weight: 30%)

### 5. Character Rendering
- [ ] Stylized manga anatomy: angular jawline, sharp hair clumps
- [ ] Expressive large eyes, simplified facial planes
- [ ] Clear emotional acting, tense gesture lines
- [ ] Not Western comic faces or realistic portraits

### 6. Robot Rendering
- [ ] Crisp technical detail with hard black shadows and white highlights
- [ ] Mechanical line accents, panel cuts
- [ ] Some details disappear into black/white — not every contour drawn
- [ ] Not realistic 3D shading or fully rendered

### 7. Page/Composition Structure
- [ ] Dynamic — strong directional flow
- [ ] Speed lines radiate from action/impact points logically
- [ ] At least one strong diagonal in the composition
- [ ] Final/most important area has highest contrast

## TECHNICAL QUALITY (Weight: 20%)

### 8. AI Artifacts
- [ ] No mangled anatomy or impossible poses
- [ ] No distorted screentone patterns
- [ ] Clean, consistent ink rendering

### 9. Contrast Management
- [ ] Stark contrast between blacks and whites
- [ ] The focal point has the highest contrast
- [ ] Not muddy, grey, or low-contrast

### 10. Technical Consistency
- [ ] Line weight is intentional and varied appropriately
- [ ] Screentone spacing is even and consistent
- [ ] No mixed rendering styles (part manga, part Western)

## CONTENT ACCURACY (Weight: 15%)

### 11. Subject Correctness
- [ ] Characters, robots, and scene elements match the description
- [ ] No Western comic elements (speech bubbles, sound effects, cover furniture)
- [ ] Manga speech bubbles if any (not Western style)

### 12. Mood & Energy
- [ ] Sharp, kinetic, high-contrast, disciplined, expressive
- [ ] Tension, speed, graphic abstraction
- [ ] Not static or boring

## SCORING

- **PASS:** ≥ 9/12 AND black & white gate check passed
- **REVISE:** 6-8/12 or mostly B&W with minor color bleed
- **FAIL:** < 6/12 or color present
