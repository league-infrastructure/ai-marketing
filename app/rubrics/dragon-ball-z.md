# Evaluation Rubric: Dragon Ball Z Anime

## STYLE FIDELITY (Weight: 35%)

### 1. Toriyama Art Style (Pass/Fail — gate check)
- [ ] Do characters have Toriyama's distinctive facial style: angular eyes, defined brow
  ridges, small nose, strong jawlines?
- [ ] Is the hair large, spiky, multi-directional, and gravity-defying?
- **FAIL if:** Characters look like generic anime or different anime style entirely

### 2. Cel Shading
- [ ] Hard-edged cel shading with sharp transitions between light and shadow
- [ ] Muscles defined by angular shadow blocks
- [ ] Anime-style specular highlights: sharp white dashes on eyes and hair
- [ ] No soft gradients, airbrush, or painterly rendering

### 3. Color Palette
- [ ] Saturated primary/secondary colors characteristic of DBZ
- [ ] Bright orange/blue for gi, warm skin tones, vibrant greens
- [ ] Gold for Super Saiyan hair (if applicable), electric blue for ki auras
- [ ] Not muted, desaturated, or pastel

### 4. Era Authenticity
- [ ] Resembles 1990s Toei Animation cel animation, not modern digital anime
- [ ] No glossy 2010s+ anime rendering
- [ ] No Dragon Ball Super: Super Hero 3D CGI style

## COMPOSITION (Weight: 30%)

### 5. Energy Effects
- [ ] Ki auras, speed lines, impact bursts, or dust clouds present
- [ ] Energy effects are dramatic and well-integrated
- [ ] Action scenes have motion blur streaks and impact frames

### 6. Dynamic Posing
- [ ] Characters in dynamic, weight-forward stances
- [ ] Arms ready, intense facial expressions
- [ ] Low-angle or slightly tilted camera — never dead-flat horizontal

### 7. Background
- [ ] Simple background: sky, clouds, or abstract energy field
- [ ] Not detailed realistic environments
- [ ] Background serves the action, not competes with it

## TECHNICAL QUALITY (Weight: 20%)

### 8. AI Artifacts
- [ ] No mangled anatomy — DBZ anatomy is exaggerated but internally consistent
- [ ] Hair spikes are coherent, not a blob
- [ ] Energy auras are clean, not a mess of artifacts

### 9. Character Consistency
- [ ] All characters in the same DBZ style — not mixed anime styles
- [ ] Proportions are Toriyama-appropriate (muscular, sturdy, not lanky)
- [ ] If multiple characters, they look like they belong in the same universe

### 10. Robot/Mech Style
- [ ] Mechanical elements use Toriyama's rounded-capsule aesthetic
- [ ] Chrome/metallic surfaces with cel-shaded highlight blocks
- [ ] Not realistic engineering drawings or hyper-detailed mechs

## CONTENT ACCURACY (Weight: 15%)

### 11. Subject Correctness
- [ ] Correct characters, poses, and scene elements
- [ ] Action matches the described scene
- [ ] No elements from wrong anime series

### 12. Mood & Energy
- [ ] High-intensity, dramatic, exciting
- [ ] The DBZ spirit of pushing limits and epic confrontation
- [ ] Not boring, static, or low-energy

## SCORING

- **PASS:** ≥ 9/12 AND Toriyama style gate check passed
- **REVISE:** 6-8/12 or close but missing key DBZ markers
- **FAIL:** < 6/12 or wrong anime style
