# Graphic Novel — Negative Prompt

Do NOT include any of the following:

Flat, un-shaded cel fills or a vintage four-color newsprint look — this style is fully
rendered with dimensional shading. Ben-Day dots, halftone patterns, or visible print-dot
texture. Washed-out, desaturated, muddy, or pastel color.

Photorealism, photographic faces, 3D render, CGI, or an untouched photo that has not been
redrawn as illustration. Loose sketch, unfinished linework, or scribbly rendering.
Anime/manga cel style, chibi, Funko-Pop or vinyl-toy proportions, oversized heads, or
cartoon-merchandising cuteness.

Invented posters, signs, slogans, labels, or extra lettering beyond the exact text specified.
Text or code on walls, clothing, or any surface other than a front-facing screen.

Weapons, lasers, explosions, or military imagery.
