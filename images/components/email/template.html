Student Spotlight  •  June 2026

The robot Marcus built can find its own way across the course.

Every week at The League, a student turns an idea into a machine that actually works. This month, twelve-year-old Marcus designed, wired, and programmed his first autonomous rover — then spent three sessions debugging the code line by line until the robot rolled cleanly through the challenge mat on its own.

That moment — when the robot finally does what you told it to — is the whole point. Behind every one of these builds is a young programmer learning to think like an engineer: patient, precise, and unafraid to fail forward.

Your kid could be next.

The League runs hands-on robotics teams and coding classes for students in grades 4–12. No experience needed — just curiosity. Come join the team, sign up for a class, and be part of what we’re building.
